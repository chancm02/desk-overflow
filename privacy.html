Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Desk Overflow is an Iowa registered trade name operated by a sole proprietor based in Vinton, Iowa. This policy explains how information submitted through this website is handled. It does not replace a separate service agreement or customer-specific data-processing terms.

Information we collect

When you request information, we ask for your name, business name, email address, phone number, type of business, service interests and any details you choose to share. Technical hosting and form-processing providers may also collect ordinary connection data needed to operate and protect their services.

How we use it

We use inquiry details to respond to your request, understand your business needs, arrange a consultation, prepare a service proposal and maintain records of our communications. Submitting this form does not sign you up for marketing text messages or automated promotional calls.

Form processing and sharing

At present, the inquiry page prepares an email using your own email application. You must press Send there for it to reach us; completing the form itself does not transmit it. The public site does not currently operate an automatic submission or payment checkout. If a server-based intake is introduced, this policy will be updated to describe where inquiry data is transmitted, stored and retained before that change is activated. We may share information with service providers strictly as needed for inquiries or contracted services. We do not sell inquiry details.

Security and retention

No online transmission is entirely risk-free. Please do not send payment card numbers, health records, passwords or other sensitive personal data in this form. We keep inquiries only as long as reasonably needed for legitimate business, operational and legal purposes. Requests for access, correction or deletion are handled as applicable law requires.

Telephone, SMS and AI services

Businesses that later engage Desk Overflow will review their own call-handling, recording, consent, texting, and data-retention requirements during onboarding. Texting and automated calling are not activated merely by submitting an inquiry. Service-specific terms and disclosures are established before deployment.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact Desk Overflow through the website inquiry form or call (319) 536-4100. Based in Vinton, Iowa, United States.

Updates

We may revise this policy as our services evolve. The effective date above identifies the current version.
