What happens after I choose?

You provide business details and review the proposed scope. We confirm platform and phone compatibility, configure the agreed call flow, test it, and get your Go-Live approval. The website currently accepts inquiries; online payment and automated account creation are not active yet.

What does implementation cover?

Basic covers the inbound receptionist, approved FAQ content, email notices, one routing/fallback flow and tests. Premium adds its included calendar and CRM configuration. Advanced also adds its included chat, email and review workflows. Each includes one consolidated revision round. You supply approved content, authorized access and sign-off; custom integrations, migration and extra locations are separate.

What does support include?

Named access, platform-fault support, one launch checklist review and two factual information updates per monthly period. Additional design/training and new workflows are separately scoped. Support review hours: weekdays 6–9 p.m. Central, excluding holidays; initial reply target two business days. This is not emergency dispatch or a 24/7 staffed support desk.

How do limits work?

Voice allowances apply per monthly service period and do not roll over. Minutes measure connected call time, not caller count. A 250-minute booster is listed at $99 once, but it is not for sale until metering, payment crediting and refund handling are connected and tested. No automatic paid overages. The limit behavior and call fallback must be tested and approved before any service goes live.
