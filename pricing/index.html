Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you commit.

When do I start paying monthly?

The $500 setup fee is invoiced after a signed order and feasibility review. The $499 monthly service starts at approved go-live after testing, then renews monthly. Your inquiry does not start billing.

Can I add services later?

Yes. Add-ons are separately scoped and quoted. Availability depends on compatible platforms, compliance requirements and any usage-based costs.

Will my business phone number change?

Call forwarding, number configuration and routing are part of the setup plan. We verify what is possible with your existing carrier before changing anything.

Can the AI guarantee every booking?

No. Direct calendar booking is a separate add-on and must be tested with your calendar. The starter package captures appointment requests for your team to follow up.

How do payments work?

After reviewing the proposed scope and signing the agreement, we use Stripe-hosted payment instructions or invoices. Live checkout is activated only when your order is approved.
