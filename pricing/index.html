COMMON QUESTIONS

Understand the details before you commit.

The commercial terms are confirmed in the actual agreement and order form.

Can I keep my business phone number?

Often, yes. We check your phone provider's supported forwarding and routing methods before configuration.

Can it book directly into my calendar?

When a supported calendar is connected and tested with your approved rules. Unverified integrations are never promised.

What if the AI makes a mistake?

AI systems can make mistakes. We set approved information and escalation rules, conduct testing, and make changes as needed. No guarantee of uninterrupted coverage or outcomes.

Are texting and outbound AI calls included?

They're not enabled by default. SMS requires separate consent, applicable registration and compliance reviews; outbound promotional AI calls are excluded from the initial offering.

When do I get billed?

The proposed structure is setup and first month prepaid, with ongoing subscription billing aligned to approved Go-Live. Final terms appear in the signed order.

Is it month-to-month?

The proposed service is month-to-month with cancellation terms specified in the signed agreement.
