QUESTIONS & REALISTIC EXPECTATIONS

Know what to expect.

Can I keep my existing business number?

Often, yes. We first check whether your carrier and phone system can forward unanswered, busy or after-hours calls.

Can the AI book directly into my calendar?

Yes, when a supported calendar is connected and booking rules, availability and permissions have been tested. We do not invent appointment availability.

Do you offer texting yet?

Texting is planned, not a standard feature today. Business SMS requires provisioning, messaging registration and appropriate consent.

Does every call become a booking?

No. Some inquiries aren't qualified and results vary. The aim is to respond more consistently and capture details your team can use.

How quickly can we launch?

It depends on your current phone routing, calendar setup, business information and testing. We provide a realistic timeline during scoping.
