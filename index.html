Common questions

Know what you're getting.

Can I keep my current business phone number?

Often. We first assess whether your phone carrier supports conditional forwarding for unanswered, busy or after-hours calls. Your existing number and routing are reviewed before launch.

Can the AI schedule directly into my calendar?

Calendar integration is available where the calendar provider and permissions are supported. We test the agreed booking rules before you authorize live booking.

Does the AI replace my employees?

No. It's designed for routine caller assistance and lead collection. You define which situations need staff follow-up, human transfer or alternative routing.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

AI can make mistakes. We set approved answers and fallback rules, test the system before launch, and provide a process for updating the configuration. Results and uninterrupted availability are not guaranteed.

Do you support text messaging?

SMS may be considered separately, but it is not activated until applicable consent, carrier registration, and required approvals are complete.

Am I signing a long-term contract?

Our proposed plans are month-to-month, with cancellation terms described in the signed service agreement. The implementation scope and terms are reviewed before payment.
