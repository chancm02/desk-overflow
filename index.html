Common questions

Questions worth asking first.

Do I have to change my existing business phone number?

Often, no. Many phone providers support forwarding calls when you're closed, busy or don't answer. The exact setup depends on your carrier, phone system and routing capabilities, and we verify that before launch.

Can the AI book directly into my calendar?

Yes, with a properly configured supported calendar, availability rules and booking permissions. If you don't already use Google or Outlook, we can assess a dedicated scheduling calendar instead. The AI should never invent appointment availability.

Will this replace my employees or answer every question?

The goal is to handle routine intake and help staff focus on the conversations that need them. We define what the AI can say and when to transfer or request human follow-up. No AI is perfect, so testing and ongoing review matter.

Can you handle emergencies and high-priority callers?

We can configure screening questions and escalation actions based on your business rules. A live human handoff requires a reachable destination. Desk Overflow should not be used as an emergency dispatch or public-safety service.

Do you offer missed-call text-back and SMS reminders?

SMS is a planned optional capability, not included by default today. U.S. business texting can require messaging registration, documented consent, phone provisioning and campaign approval. We'll discuss readiness and costs before offering it.

Can an AI agent call customers back?

Supported AI platforms can make certain outbound calls, but availability depends on your specific plan and configuration. Promotional or automated outreach requires a compliance review and appropriate consent. We do not activate outbound campaigns without those steps.

How quickly can I get started?

Timing depends on phone forwarding, business information, calendar integration, testing and any provider approvals. We will outline the steps and a realistic launch timeline after reviewing your setup—rather than promise a one-day deployment.
