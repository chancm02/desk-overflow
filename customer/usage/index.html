Your included allowance

Basic includes 150, Premium 250 and Advanced 500 AI-connected voice minutes per monthly service period. No rollover or automatic paid overages. Your signed order defines the meter, rounding, transfers and exclusions.

Unlimited AI and telephone minutes

Unlimited AI is not unlimited telephone service. HighLevel’s eligible Unlimited AI subscription remains subject to provider fair use and excludes telephone charges and some AI products. Advanced currently has a 500-minute allowance; Unlimited AI will only be offered after its provider subscription, supported features and customer-specific terms are confirmed. It is not active from selecting a plan here.

Alerts and spending protection

Before launch, we must test customer and Desk Overflow notifications at 70%, 80%, 90% and 100% of the agreed allowance, plus your chosen fallback at the limit. Provider spending alerts and wallet balances are separate from your plan’s minute allowance, can be delayed and may use calendar months rather than your billing period.

Extra spending requires approval by default. Any future automatic top-up option must have separate consent, a stated purchase amount, a monthly maximum and an easy way to disable it. No such option is currently enabled. A website setting cannot guarantee an instantaneous carrier cutoff.

Before your number goes live

Desk Overflow must verify metering, billing-period boundaries, alerts, routing and your approved fallback on the actual account. If a reliable control cannot be configured, we resolve the scope before activation. Your fallback may route calls to your existing number or voicemail, according to the tested configuration.

Need an account-specific answer?

Support verifies your business contact before sharing private data. A usage statement should include the service period, data timestamp, included and purchased minutes, consumed and remaining minutes, and any approved charges.
