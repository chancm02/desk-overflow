## Primary sources checked October 21, 2026
- [Iowa Code 808B.2](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/808B.2.pdf): includes exceptions for a party or consent from a party under specified circumstances. This alone does not settle interstate caller requirements, AI disclosures or a particular platform's processing. Draft requires review of actual caller locations and settings rather than a blanket one-party-consent claim.
- [Iowa electronic transactions chapter 554D](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/554D.html): addresses electronic records, signatures and attribution. Verify signer authority, consent, transaction scope and retained audit record; do not equate a link click with an executed agreement.
- [Iowa taxable services](https://revenue.iowa.gov/taxes/tax-guidance/sales-use-excise-tax/iowa-sales-and-use-tax-taxable-services) and [digital products/software guidance](https://revenue.iowa.gov/taxes/tax-guidance/sales-use-excise-tax/taxation-specified-digital-products-software-and-related-services): classification matters. Obtain a determination for the actual managed AI receptionist scope and customer location. No tax-exempt or taxable conclusion is made here.
- [FCC AI voice declaratory ruling FCC 24-17](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-17A1.pdf): AI-generated voice falls within artificial/prerecorded voice restrictions for covered calls. Launch excludes automated promotional outbound AI calls and texting; do not infer that inbound processing needs no other legal review.

These sources identify review topics. They are not a complete survey of all applicable laws, later interpretations or customer jurisdictions. Counsel should determine additional requirements before customer execution and service activation.
