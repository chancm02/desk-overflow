## Document 1 Managed receptionist service agreement

### Parties and effective date
This agreement is between [PROVIDER LEGAL NAME], doing business as Desk Overflow, with an address at [PROVIDER ADDRESS] (Provider), and [CUSTOMER LEGAL NAME], with an address at [CUSTOMER ADDRESS] (Customer). It takes effect on [EFFECTIVE DATE] when both parties sign. Each signer confirms authority to bind the party identified below. Website inquiries alone do not create this agreement.

### Services and order of documents
Provider will configure and manage the inbound AI receptionist services described in the signed Service Order. The Data and Call Handling Addendum applies to Customer Data. The Service Order controls specific scope, prices, dates and usage allowances; the Addendum controls data-handling conflicts; this agreement governs remaining matters. A change to scope, prices, allowances or billing requires a written change approved by both parties. A platform setting or AI statement cannot amend this agreement.

The initial service covers approved after-hours or missed-call answering, lead capture, approved business FAQs, appointment scheduling where expressly configured, call summaries and human escalation tasks. Texting, automated promotional outbound calls, emergency dispatch, professional advice and handling of payment-card or sensitive regulated records are excluded. Provider may decline unsupported use cases before activation.

### Setup and acceptance
Customer will provide accurate business information, approved FAQ answers, authorized calendar access, business hours, service area and verified routing contacts. Customer retains ownership and control of its existing business number and carrier account unless a separate signed order expressly says otherwise. Provider will not port a number or change live forwarding without separate written approval.

Provider will configure the agreed scope, run the acceptance tests and present the results and known limitations to Customer. Live activation requires a signed acceptance record, verified fallback, approved cost controls and separate written approval from both parties. No activation date is guaranteed until these prerequisites are satisfied. Customer must review proposed answers and routing promptly and report incorrect results.

### Fees and payment
The setup fee is $500 once. Managed service is $499 per month. The Service Order identifies included minutes, any approved extras, applicable tax treatment and the first recurring billing date. Setup is invoiced after signature. Recurring service starts on the mutually approved activation date; it does not start merely because Customer submits an inquiry, signs this agreement or pays setup. If activation is delayed, the parties will confirm a revised recurring start date before charging.

Payment is collected through a secure HighLevel-supported gateway. Provider will not request card numbers or security codes in intake forms, email, call notes or the knowledge base. Recurring automatic payment requires the separate authorization. No automatic excess-usage charge or paid add-on is authorized by this agreement. Provider must obtain a written change before raising agreed limits or prices. Taxes will be disclosed before payment following a qualified determination of applicable tax obligations.

### Term and cancellation
PROPOSED FOR APPROVAL: service renews monthly from activation. Either party may cancel before the next renewal by written notice to the notice address in the Service Order; a notice received before the next billing date stops that renewal. No minimum annual commitment applies. Customer may revoke recurring payment authorization prospectively; revocation does not by itself eliminate amounts already properly due or change the agreed service end date. Provider will acknowledge the cancellation and confirm the final date, future invoice status, routing rollback and data export options.

PROPOSED FOR APPROVAL: completed, accepted setup work is earned. If setup ends before acceptance, Provider will supply an itemized record of work and refund the unearned portion of the $500 setup fee within 10 business days. Any first-month or prepaid amount for service that never activates will be refunded. Cancellation after activation normally takes effect at the paid period end, without a partial-period refund unless required by law or agreed in writing. An invoice or automatic charge made in error will be corrected. Any different refund arrangement must be written in the Service Order and approved before signature.

### Customer responsibilities and authorized use
Customer will maintain its carrier service, original human or voicemail fallback, staff escalation coverage and connected calendar availability. Customer authorizes only the approved access and instructions stated in the Service Order and Addendum. Customer will not ask the agent to impersonate a human, invent facts, mislead callers or collect excluded sensitive data. Each party remains responsible for its own legal obligations; neither party's approval establishes the other's legal compliance.

### Availability and support
AI can misunderstand speech, provide an inaccurate response or fail to complete a task. Third-party AI, carrier, CRM, calendar, internet and payment services may fail or change. Provider does not guarantee uninterrupted service, response times, recovered revenue, lead volume, bookings or emergency handling. Provider will use reasonable care in configuration, testing, monitoring and corrective work within the support scope agreed in the Service Order. Provider remains responsible for its own contractual obligations despite use of third parties.

When Provider identifies a material failure, it will notify the agreed contacts, investigate and use the approved rollback or fallback plan where technically possible. Customer will maintain direct access to its original carrier routing. A suspected security issue, abusive traffic, payment failure or unexpected cost spike creates a review and notification task. Where practicable, Provider will give notice and an opportunity to resolve the issue before suspension; immediate protective suspension is permitted when reasonably necessary to reduce a material security or financial risk. Provider will use the agreed human or voicemail fallback rather than intentionally discard calls, to the extent the carrier and platform permit it.

### Confidentiality and ownership
Each party will use the other's nonpublic information only to perform this agreement, restrict access to authorized personnel and approved service providers, and protect it with reasonable safeguards. These duties exclude information already public without breach, independently developed, lawfully received or required to be disclosed by law. Compelled disclosures will be limited and notified where lawful. Customer retains its business content and Customer Data. Provider retains reusable configuration methods and generic templates, without ownership of Customer Data. Customer receives access and export assistance described in the Addendum.

### Responsibility and dispute terms
PROPOSED FOR COUNSEL REVIEW: to the extent permitted by law, neither party is liable for indirect, special or consequential loss, including lost profits. Each party's total liability arising from the agreement is limited to the greater of $1,000 or the fees paid or payable under it in the three months before the event. These limits do not restrict obligations to pay proper fees, fraud, willful misconduct, or liability that law does not allow to be limited. Counsel must assess whether confidentiality, data incidents or other obligations require different limits or insurance. No blanket indemnity is included in this draft.

The parties will first seek to resolve a dispute through their named business contacts. PROPOSED FOR COUNSEL REVIEW: Iowa law governs, subject to mandatory applicable law; courts of competent jurisdiction in [AGREED COUNTY AND STATE] hear unresolved disputes. No arbitration clause or class-action waiver is included. Neither party may bind the other to a platform's terms without authority. Assignment requires the other party's written consent, except as separately agreed. If one provision is unenforceable, the remaining agreement continues to the extent lawful. Amendments must be written and accepted by both parties.
