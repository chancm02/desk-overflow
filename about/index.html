Desk Overflow is building a more practical way for service businesses to respond to callers, capture opportunities and organize human follow-up.

WHY WE EXIST

Real businesses deserve useful automation.

Business owners and crews spend their days doing the work. The phone keeps ringing. Our purpose is to help more of those callers receive an appropriate response without forcing teams into a maze of disconnected tools.

We're an Iowa-based sole proprietorship building managed AI call coverage for service teams. Every customer setup depends on reviewed requirements, an approved scope and actual technical validation.
