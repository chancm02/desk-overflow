ANSWERS BEFORE YOU START

Good questions, clear answers.

Can I keep my existing phone number?

Often, yes. We check whether your phone service supports forwarding busy, unanswered or after-hours calls before configuring the system.

Can the AI actually book appointments?

When a supported calendar is connected and the business's rules have been tested. We never promise an unverified booking integration.

What happens when a caller needs a person?

We configure agreed escalation, transfer, or callback options. Emergencies and unsupported requests require a defined fallback.

Are text messages included?

Texting is not enabled by default. Any SMS feature requires appropriate opt-in, registration and legal review.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

Our proposed packages are month-to-month, subject to the cancellation and service conditions in the customer agreement.
