# Suggested first week of organic posting

No posting or paid advertising has been scheduled. The owner decides what to publish. The final website and form must pass release checks first.

1. Introduce yourself and the offer on your business Facebook page with square-hook.jpg. Link to the website with the supplied Facebook tracking tags.
2. Post video 01 to TikTok and Facebook Reels after reviewing captions and platform-safe placement. Use a matching profile link where the platform permits it.
3. Share the example lead graphic with an explanation of the details a human needs for a callback. Keep its example label visible.
4. Participate in a relevant Reddit discussion as yourself, disclose ownership and follow that community’s rules. Use the nonpromotional question draft unless promotion is expressly allowed.
5. Post video 02 explaining the workflow and how testing/approval works.
6. Share the pricing graphic or video 03 with the complete fees, written allowance and no-auto-activation explanation.
7. Review inquiries, source tags, form failures and follow-ups. Record actual qualified inquiries and consultations; don’t invent conversion rates. Improve the weakest message based on real responses.

Review the private inbox at least daily and before/after each posting session. Email alerts are supplementary, so a failed notification must not hide a saved inquiry. Respond personally to the inquiry using the approved phone/email permission. No marketing SMS, automated promotional AI calls, scraped contact lists or unsolicited bulk messages.

Track: date/channel/creative, post URL, UTM campaign, inquiries saved, qualified conversations and next action. Keep real names, contact details and screenshots of customer records outside this public repository.

Platform guidance checked October 22, 2026: [TikTok creative quality checklist](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/Top_Tips_One_Pager_SMB.pdf) recommends vertical video and safe-area checks. [Reddit Rules](https://redditinc.com/policies/reddit-rules) require authentic participation and prohibit spam. Community-specific rules still control where promotion is allowed. No claim of ad approval or guaranteed performance is made.
